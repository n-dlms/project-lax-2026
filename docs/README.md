**Make the next money decision with the dates in view.**

Check a purchase, compare late pay, keep a work cost in your plan or test a small cushion. Electricity, groceries, transport, school fees and other essentials can all be part of the same picture.

[Try the app — no signup](https://project-lax-2026.pages.dev/)

Start on Home with **Try the R80 decision** to explore fictional numbers. Choose **Start with my own bill** when you want to use your own amounts. A saved plan can be continued in the same browser or app.
